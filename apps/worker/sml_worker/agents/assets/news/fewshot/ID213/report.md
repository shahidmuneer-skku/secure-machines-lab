# Case Summary  
On October 24, the IDF published a satellite image of 12 white tanks, claiming that the image shows fuel tanks inside Gaza containing over 500,000 liters of fuel.
We have geolocated the image and verified that it shows 12 tanks, located in Gaza close to the border with Egypt, approximately 1.2 kilometers north of the Rafah border crossing.

The satellite image the IDF has published (ID213-2) is the same satellite image found in Google Earth and Google Maps (ID213-1), dated May 2022. Details in the images, such as buildings, structures, roads, landscapes, colors and shadows, are consistent in both images and indicate that both images clearly represent the same area and were taken at the same time of day.

Our analysis therefore shows that the IDF image is not recent and was not taken in October 2023, but is a satellite image from May 2022. It is not possible to conclude from the image that the IDF has published whether the tanks contain diesel/fuel, and possibly the amount of fuel in the tanks.

# Verified Evidence  
- **Source Details** 

    On October 24 at 8:28 p.m., the IDF posted an image on its English X channel. The image is a satellite image and shows two rows of white, round tanks, a total of 12 tanks, along with the text:
    "These diesel tanks are in Gaza. They contain over 500,000 liters of fuel. Ask Hamas if you can get some." The IDF post here: https://twitter.com/IDF/status/1716884494131868138 (archived version here: https://archive.ph/mEqdI )
    The post is a response to the UN agency for Palestinian refugees, UNRWA,'s post on X the same day (17:45) warning of an urgent need for fuel. According to the UN, they will have to close hospitals in Gaza on the evening of October 25 if they do not receive fuel.
    Post UNRWA: https://twitter.com/UNRWA/status/1716843075211792558

    IDF spokesperson Avichay Adraee also posted similar images and claims on X: https://twitter.com/AvichayAdraee/status/1716705535175631198 (archived version here: https://archive.ph/Sbntd )
    "This is what over half a million liters of diesel in Hamas's possession looks like." He further refers to the claims by the Gaza/Hamas authorities that there is a shortage of diesel for hospitals, bakeries and civilians. The IDF spokesperson writes that Hamas steals diesel from civilians and transfers this to its tunnels and weapons.

- **Other Info:** 

    Here's what we know about the tanks:
    Using the Google Earth measurement tool, we estimate the diameter of the tanks to be approximately 12 meters. If a twelve-meter diameter tank is five meters high, then such a tank can hold over 500,000 liters, according to Crown Oil Environmental's tank calculator.

    Going back in time, we see that the tanks were put in place sometime after July 2020. In May 2021, we see black circles, indicating that the tanks were under construction in 2021.
    During the period after May 2021, but before May 2022, a fence has also been constructed around the area where the tanks are located.
