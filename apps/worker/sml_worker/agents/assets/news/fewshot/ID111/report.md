# Case Summary

Title: Video shows attack on what may be Norwegian-supplied artillery
PARTIALLY VERIFIED

The video shows three artillery vehicles being bombarded with what appears to be cluster weapons or dense artillery fire.

There are several claims that Faktisk Verifiserbar cannot verify, including whether it is Norwegian-supplied artillery or if it was destroyed. We have geolocated the video to an agricultural area in Donetsk Oblast. We consider that the video was likely filmed on June 6.

# Content Classification

- **Tags:** Artillery, Ukraine War

# Verified Evidence

- **Source Details:**

The video is shared by a TV channel affiliated with the Russian military. The channel's logo is embedded in the corner of the clip. This suggests that the channel may be the origin of the video, as we do not find the same clip without this watermark.

Article from tvzvezda.ru: [<u>Накрыть артиллерией: кадры поражения поставленных Норвегией украинских САУ - ТРК Звезда Новости, 07.06.2022</u>](https://tvzvezda.ru/news/202267153-5Kiup.html)

In the video, you can see three Ukrainian artillery vehicles coming under heavy artillery fire. There are dozens of explosions around the vehicles, and you can see a fire breaking out near one of the vehicles. There is too much smoke to clearly see the extent of the damage.

Faktisk Verifiserbar believes the video likely shows self-propelled artillery vehicles of the M109 type. This is because the vehicles have the gun turret at the back, and they have a characteristic gun muzzle.

From the article:

_The Russian Ministry of Defense showed footage of the defeat by Russian artillery of self-propelled artillery installations of the Armed Forces of Ukraine M109, supplied to Ukraine by Norway._

They also refer to a press release earlier the same day from the Russian Ministry of Defense, claiming that the Russians managed to destroy a Norwegian M109 vehicle.

Igor Konashenkov, spokesperson for the Russian Ministry of Defense, reported this:

_"As a result of artillery strikes, one 155-mm M109A3 self-propelled gun transferred by Norway, two 155-mm M777 howitzers made in the United States, as well as seven field artillery pieces and mortars, including a 203-mm Pion self-propelled gun, were destroyed,"_

The video is also circulating among military bloggers on Twitter.

<img src="./report/image1.png" style="width:6.09375in;height:4.75in" />

Here it is claimed that the Russians used cluster munitions in the attack. The extent of the damage after the incident is also widely discussed. Many believe that the vehicle is unlikely to be destroyed, as it seems to be burning in the area behind the vehicle. Thus, probably gunpowder charges lying a bit behind.

Faktisk Verifiserbar cannot determine that the artillery vehicle was destroyed - “destroyed,” as the Russian Ministry of Defense says - based on what we see in the video. There is too much smoke for that. It also seems to be burning only in the area behind the vehicle.

In an information war, one might question whether it is conspicuous that the video ends before the smoke clears, so that details and the extent of the damage cannot be seen.

The video clip is geolocated to an agricultural area in Donetsk Oblast. Since the message did not contain an alleged location for the clip, we had to search along the entire front in the Donetsk region. Fortunately, the fields have a characteristic shape that allowed us to find the right place.

This is approximately 30 kilometers southwest of Sievierodonetsk, according to Google Earth.

- **When? (Time):**

Time: Likely June 6

On June 7 at 10:35, a spokesperson for <span class="mark">the Russian Ministry of Defense</span>, Major General Igor Konashenkov, reported that Russia had knocked out a Norwegian-supplied artillery of the type M109A3. A few hours later, this video appeared on the website of TV Zvezda. TV Zvezda is a Russian TV network operated by the Russian Ministry of Defense. https://tvzvezda.ru/news/202267153-5Kiup.html

Likely June 6, the day before the daily press conference and the publication of the video.

Assuming they are the Norwegian vehicles, it is unlikely that the Russians would have waited to publish a video of them destroying NATO equipment.

There has been bright sunshine in the relevant area since the vehicles arrived in Ukraine, which seems to match the weather in the video.

Reverse image search of the thumbnail yields no hits on Tineye, while naturally information about “similar images” of military vehicles in nature appears on other search engines - but without direct hits.
